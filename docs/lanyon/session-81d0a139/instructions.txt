Run the heat-diffusion solver with alpha = 1 on 100 uniform cells over x in [0, 1], with u(0) = 0 and u(1) = 1 held fixed, from the initial condition u(x, 0) = sin(pi * x) + x, to a short final time t = 0.1 with a stable time step.

Report the solution profile u(x) at the final time (cell centres and values), the minimum and maximum of u over the domain at the final time, and the maximum difference from the exact solution u(x, t) = exp(-pi^2 * t) * sin(pi * x) + x at t = 0.1.

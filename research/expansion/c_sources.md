# Expansion C: six industries, eighteen distinct profiles

Reviewed 2026-10-31. Twelve profiles and eleven instrument records are new; three materials and three quantum profiles are existing reviewed seeds. The six industry rows in `c_manifest.json` each select exactly three experiments and three real configurations for the parent integration's actual Lean evaluation. Inclusion in an industry is a comparison choice, never a suitability assertion.

Each new profile includes an essential instrument-function requirement. Function flags use open-world semantics: an undocumented function remains UNKNOWN. No unlisted function is inferred absent, and no equipment modification is represented as already installed. Quantities are exact decimal strings. No Lean proof, solver rule or acceptance condition was changed.

## Energy and hydrogen

- [NASA CTL instrument sheet, 2015](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20150011030/downloads/20150011030.pdf?attachment=true): Cryostat-100 is a real cylindrical absolute insulation calorimeter. The profile checks the standard 78 K cold and 293 K warm boundaries separately, plus boiloff calorimetry. This prevents a generic cold stage from satisfying the experiment.
- [Micromeritics application note 136](https://downloads.micromeritics.com/Appnotes/Using-the-ASAP-2020-for-Determining-the-Hydrogen-Adsorption-Capacity-of-Powders-and-Porous-Materials.pdf): hydrogen sorption uses the ASAP 2020's volumetric analysis, prepared powder tube, cryogenic bath and free-space correction. The selected profile is the LN2 implementation of a documented method class. Its specimen temperature remains unspecified; the bath does not prove an exact sample setpoint.
- [NPL facility description](https://www.npl.co.uk/products-services/advanced-materials/cryogenic-testing-services): actual helium-cooled mechanical testing supports hydrogen-related conditions around 20 K. Tensile loading is distinct from heat-flow and molecular-adsorption measurements. Load capacity, grips, strain rate and temperature uniformity require facility-specific review; no strength result is claimed.

## Metrology and calibration

- [Pan et al., 2021, full author manuscript](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=931816): actual LCM-LNE-Cnam apparatus uses a copper helium quasi-sphere, a Cryomech PT405 cooler, acoustic/microwave readout and resistance bridges. The profile checks acoustic thermometry, bridge readout and the observed 30–120 kPa pressure series. The measured neon thermodynamic temperature is an experimental result, not a control range or exact AGT setpoint. The equipment temperature range is explicitly unknown.
- [INRIM laboratory description](https://www.inrim.it/en/research/scientific-sectors/quantum-electronics/laboratories/quantum-voltage-standard-laboratory-0): the actual liquid-helium PJVS operates at 4.2 K and produces quantized DC reference voltages up to 10 V. The separate pulse-driven AC/JAWS platform is excluded. Compatibility does not certify an uncertainty or calibration.
- [Houston and Livigni, 2001, DOI10.6028/jres.106.029](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4862825/): NIST's LOCR, specifically, holds its absorber near 5.4 K and accepts a horizontal beam for electrical-substitution radiometry and photodiode-trap calibration. HACR has a different optical geometry; the LOCR temperature is not attributed to HACR. The historical apparatus is a documented research configuration; current booking availability is unverified.

## Photonics and telecommunications

- [Vyhnalek et al., NASA 2018](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20180002074/downloads/20180002074.pdf): a real two-detector Opus One system at 2.5 K was characterized with an external calibrated 1550 nm optical path and TCSPC. Those external instruments are part of the named published setup, not automatically installed on every Opus One. The scene shows detector hotspots, electrical pulses and counting, not an invented efficiency verdict.
- [Thomas et al., Science Advances2024, DOI10.1126/sciadv.adi7346, full text](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC11014446/fullTextXML): the inspected methods place the quantum-dot source at 4 K in an attoDRY800, with pulsed excitation, internal objective, piezo positioning, fibre splitting, external SNSPD detection and a time tagger. The selected profile checks the source/HBT subsystem; it excludes the warm rubidium memory. A4 K source cryostat is not asserted to cool the external photon detectors.
- [Wiens et al.,2020, DOI10.1063/1.5140321, full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2004.01552v3): the actual Leiden CF-1K/Joule-Thomson setup contains a 50 mm vertical silicon resonator and optical breadboard, operated across1.5–3.6 K. The apparatus measures line centers using scanning/detuning interrogation; the paper explicitly says the cryogenic PDH lock was not implemented. Measured drift and stability are not encoded as guaranteed compatibility outcomes.

## Semiconductors and electronics

- [Zhang et al.,2021, full preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2104.03094): native silicon MOSFET DC transfer/output measurements used a liquid-nitrogen/helium Dewar and B1500A analyzer across4.2–300 K. The profile therefore requires an analyzer, not just cold probe arms. A catalog station does not acquire an analyzer by appearing in the same industry.
- [Scheffler et al.,2023, DOI10.1002/pssa.202200633, full article](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/pssa.202200633): capacitance-transient defect spectroscopy used contacted nitrogen-containing float-zone silicon diodes and a closed-cycle helium cryostat across36–300 K. The separate optically excited MCTS apparatus is not merged into the DLTS profile. Pulse/readout functionality remains mandatory.
- [Lake Shore FastHall catalog, revision062526](https://www.lakeshore.com/docs/default-source/default-document-library/fasthall-station.pdf?sfvrsn=5c63af14_24): the real installed LN2 option includes a flooded bath, reservoir and 0.75 T wide-gap magnet. The profile is a documented method class for four-contact van der Pauw Hall analysis, not a fabricated material dataset. The catalog does not guarantee sample temperature, so no exact temperature range is invented. Gate bias is an additional option and is excluded.
- [Lake Shore CPX specifications](https://www.lakeshore.com/products/categories/specification/material-characterization-products/cryogenic-probe-stations/model-cpx-cryogenic-probe-station): standard control is4.4–475 K, with an included31.8 mm holder. Lower-temperature stages and microwave/fibre probes are optional. Maximum six-arm capacity is not an installed count. External SMU/analyzer and DLTS electronics remain unknown. The third selected option is the existing reviewed CRX-VF configuration, physically distinct through its closed-cycle cooler and vertical-field magnet.

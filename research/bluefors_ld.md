# Bluefors LD-series dilution refrigerators (LD250 / LD400) — documented manufacturer specifications

Compiled 2026-10-31. Every value below was read on a page/document fetched in this session; anything not found is marked UNKNOWN.

## IMPORTANT: model renaming (March 2026)

Bluefors renamed the LD250 to **LD350** and the LD400 / LD400sl to **LD450 / LD450sl** on 2026-03-10 and raised the guaranteed cooling-power specs at the same time. The current bluefors.com product pages therefore carry LD350/LD450 specs only. The original LD250/LD400 guaranteed specs were found in the Bluefors "User Manual LD System" (v2.0, Jan 2022), a Bluefors document hosted by Aalto University. Both generations are recorded below with their applies_to labels.

- value: LD250 → LD350; LD400 / LD400sl → LD450 / LD450sl; XLD400sl → XLD450sl

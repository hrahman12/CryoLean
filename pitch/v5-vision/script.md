# CryoLean v5 · Vision and ask: 60-second talk track

**Slide 1 · Title (about 15 s).** We're CryoLean. Every cryogenics lab asks the same question: can this cryostat run my experiment? We made the answer simple: twenty industries, 220 machine setups, and one Lean checker that decides.

**Slide 2 · Scale (about 22 s).** Here are 180 featured pairings, one row per industry. Every square is a Lean verdict: 134 met, 26 need more information, 20 do not meet. Across the whole catalog Lean decided 44,880 experiment-machine pairs, backed by 3,335 sourced records. Adding a new industry is just new data; the Lean core stayed unchanged, and a theorem proves the label can't change a verdict.

**Slide 3 · What we ask (about 23 s).** So we ask two things. Cryostat makers: give us sourced, machine-readable specs, because 665 of 2,832 capability entries are still unknown. Labs: send your real experiment needs. Next, we prove the five coupled physics models still unsupported. Try it now at hrahman12.github.io/CryoLean.

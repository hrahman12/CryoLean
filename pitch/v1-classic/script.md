# CryoLean v1 · Classic: 60-second talk track

**1 · Title (10 s).** Every cryogenics lab asks the same question: can this cryostat do my experiment? CryoLean answers it. AI suggests, Lean decides, Z3 explains.

**2 · The problem (15 s).** A cryostat's limits are fixed, and every experiment wants something different. A Bluefors fridge takes 24 hours to get cold, so a mismatch found there costs that day again. Today people match specs by hand, and almost a quarter of the specs are unconfirmed.

**3 · What it does (20 s).** Ask whether a Bluefors LD400 can run 50-qubit wiring. Each spec passes: cold enough, enough lines. But together the lines put 14.6 microwatts into a stage that removes 12. Lean says no and tells you why. Z3 suggests 39 flux lines, and Lean re-checks: requirements met.

**4 · Proof it works (15 s).** Every verdict comes from a theorem: 128 of them, no sorry. We injected 2,750 faults and none got through. Proofs cover our model, not the physical machine. Thank you.
